How it works
From struggle to mastery
01 — Your session

Built around what
you're about to forget.

Memory fades on a schedule, whether you track it or not. Catalyst does — it knows which concepts are slipping before you'd notice yourself, and brings them back right when you're about to lose them. You don't have to plan your own revision or guess what to revisit. Just show up — your path to mastery is already laid out.

02 — In session

Designed to find what you think you understand.

Every question targets a specific edge — the concept you half-grasp, the assumption you've been papering over. Questions build on each other within a topic, each one resting on the last, so a shaky foundation gets caught before it's buried under everything stacked on top of it. You don't just see what you missed — you see exactly where your understanding gives way.

03 — After every session

It didn't just score you.
It found why.

A bad score rarely means the topic is hard — it usually means something from weeks ago never fully clicked, and everything you built on top of it has been wobbling ever since. Catalyst traces root causes, maps the dependency chain, and adds the fix to tomorrow's session automatically.
